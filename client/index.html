Xerxes Data Centers

Power from below. Intelligence above.

Xerxes builds next-generation data centers with geothermal energy embedded in their foundations — off-grid, carbon-negative and engineered for up to 1.5 ExaFLOPS of sovereign AI compute. The DTF (Dark Token Factory) is the small model; Xerxes is the Goliath.

Models

DTF — Dark Token Factory: a sealed, lights-out, robot-serviced AI factory on one re-entered well, with four branches growing to ten (darktokenfactory.com). Xerxes: a central hub and six compute pods under a nanocomposite shell roof, over twelve wells — two under every pod, fifty metres apart — with ten directional branches per well, 120 in total, each reaching about five kilometres deep.

Technology

Enhanced Quantum Geothermal (EQG): closed-loop wells with nanomaterial-enhanced pipework and a design PUE of 1.05. The nano-shell roof captures wind and cold for the chillers, and rack heat is recovered and reused. Built for NVIDIA Vera Rubin, next-generation AMD Instinct GPUs, advanced TPUs, NPUs and new high-density chips at 150 kW per rack.

Programme

The KSA programme is on standby: regional conflict and instability have slowed delivery. Xerxes expects to resume with its partners once conditions allow. No dates are committed.
